# Advisory coaching-quality rubric

Layer C may score each dimension from 1 to 5. It is advisory and never overrides deterministic or Layer B failures.

- Specificity: cites observable details from this take.
- Actionability: gives a concrete next-session adjustment.
- Kindness without flattery: respectful, direct, and free of invented praise.
- Grounding: does not invent words, events, or measurements.
- No identity judgement: avoids conclusions about accent, gender, identity, or personality.
